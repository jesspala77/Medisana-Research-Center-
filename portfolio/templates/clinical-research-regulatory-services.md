## Delivery Notes

- Keep patient information out of staff CVs and public-facing templates.
- Treat SynNexus tracker items as operational coordination records, not final regulatory determinations.
- Keep final submission decisions with the investigator, site regulatory lead, IRB, sponsor, or CRO as appropriate.
- Save client-specific deliverables in the client workspace or document storage location.
