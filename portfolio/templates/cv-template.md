## Review Notes

- Keep patient information out of staff CVs.
- Use candidate clinical data only inside the clinical-recruitment workflow, not in the public CV body.
- Keep credentials current before sponsor, CRO, IRB, or audit submission.
- Save final PDFs and credential packet source files in the appropriate portfolio or document storage location.
